01 — About

Systems that people actually depend on

I'm a full-stack developer with over a year of production experience building and maintaining business systems across payroll, biometric attendance, event ticketing, fleet logistics, pharmacy operations and mobile sales analytics.

I work mainly in React, TypeScript, Node.js and PostgreSQL, with Flutter on the mobile side. I care most about the unglamorous parts: correct data models, permissions enforced server-side, and real-time state that doesn't drift.

I hold a Bachelor of Information and Communications Technology from Durban University of Technology.

  • Security first

    Role-based access enforced server-side, not just hidden in the UI. JWT, RBAC, row-level security.

  • Real-time by default

    WebSocket-driven state that stays in sync across modules — and cache keys that don't go stale.

  • Tested and reviewed

    Jest, Vitest and React Testing Library across the stack, plus reviewing teammates' code on the platform I lead.
